# Media87 Website Project Instructions

This workspace is the source of truth for rebuilding Media87 as a fast, expandable, search-led website.

## Start Here

Before planning, writing, designing, or implementing pages, read:

1. `README.md`
2. `docs/MEDIA87-SEO-CONTENT-STANDARD.md`
3. `docs/ARTICLE-BRIEF-TEMPLATE.md` for articles
4. `docs/PAGE-BRIEF-TEMPLATE.md` for commercial, service, product, industry, or location pages

Inspect the current live Media87 site and available first-party evidence before deciding what to preserve, rewrite, consolidate, redirect, or add. Never assume that an old page has no value merely because its design or copy is weak.

## Governing Strategy

Treat Media87 as a hybrid:

- Primary model: professional service and digital marketing agency.
- Local overlay: Dubai and the UAE, limited to truthful service coverage and real local evidence.
- Product model: ChatZen, LocalZen, and other verified Media87 products.
- Editorial model: useful articles, research, case studies, tools, and guides that support commercial pages.
- Migration lifecycle: protect valuable URLs, rankings, links, conversions, brand signals, and media during the WordPress replacement.

The desired outcome is qualified pipeline and product adoption, not traffic for its own sake.

## Non-negotiable Rules

- Do not create one page for every keyword, wording variation, city, neighbourhood, or query fan-out.
- Do not publish a location page unless it passes the location-page gate in the content standard.
- Do not invent clients, results, testimonials, awards, offices, addresses, people, credentials, prices, response times, product features, service areas, or statistics.
- Do not copy PROTRAINING facts, language preferences, programme structure, branding, or article-length requirement. Only its general research and editorial disciplines were adapted.
- Do not use fixed keyword density, geo-entity quotas, exact title/meta character limits, or a raw H1 count as ranking rules.
- Do not add a TL;DR, table of contents, FAQ, table, checklist, schema type, image, or video mechanically. Each must have a user or evidence job.
- Do not imply that FAQ or HowTo markup will earn Google rich results.
- Do not use review gating, discourage negative reviews, selectively request positive reviews, or coach customers to insert keywords into reviews.
- Do not change or remove an established URL without a documented URL map, preservation rationale, redirect, validation, and rollback note.
- Do not publish automatically. Automation may research, scaffold, draft, validate, and prepare a preview; a human must approve factual claims and production release.

## Build Requirements

- Important content, headings, links, metadata, and structured data must be present in rendered or statically generated HTML.
- Use client-side React only for components that genuinely require interaction. Do not turn the content website into a client-rendered single-page application.
- Preserve the distinctive Media87 visual identity while maintaining mobile usability, accessibility, and performance.
- Test representative pages at desktop and approximately 390 px mobile width.
- Target field Core Web Vitals at the 75th percentile: LCP at or below 2.5 seconds, INP at or below 200 milliseconds, and CLS at or below 0.1.
- Forms and primary journeys must have labels, validation, error handling, a success state, analytics events that exclude personal data, and a non-JavaScript-safe destination where practical.

## Content Workflow

Every new or materially rewritten page must move through:

1. `proposed`
2. `researched`
3. `brief-approved`
4. `draft`
5. `fact-reviewed`
6. `seo-qa`
7. `design-qa`
8. `approved`
9. `published`
10. `measured`
11. `refresh-or-consolidate`

Record unknowns as evidence requests. A missing fact is not permission to fill a template with plausible language.
