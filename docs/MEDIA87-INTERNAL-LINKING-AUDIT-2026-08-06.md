# Media87 internal-linking audit — 2026-08-06

## Result

All 30 indexable URLs are reachable from the homepage within two clicks:

- homepage: 1 page at depth 0;
- direct homepage destinations: 21 pages at depth 1;
- supporting guides and the restaurant service: 8 pages at depth 2;
- pages at depth 3 or deeper: 0;
- unreachable indexable pages: 0.

The production validator now fails if an indexable page is unreachable, requires more than three clicks, lacks an in-content link from another indexable page, or receives an in-content link from a published page while it is still a `noindex` draft.

## Contextual links added or improved

- The services hub now links directly to AI video creation, restaurant SEO and advertising, AI/LLM visibility, and the main FAQ page where each link supports the decision being discussed.
- The local SEO service links to the Dubai SEO guide, image geo-tagging tool and LocalZen reputation workflow.
- The FAQ page and AI video service link to the prompt library.
- Published AI and automation guides now cross-link to relevant approved guides, products and services.
- Published pages no longer recommend unfinished `noindex` articles in related-content sections.

## Linking standard

- Use the homepage, services hub and blog hub as the main discovery layer.
- Link from supporting articles to the most relevant commercial page when the link helps the reader take the next step.
- Link between articles only when the second page answers a genuine next question.
- Use descriptive anchor text rather than generic “click here” wording.
- Do not add links merely to reach a target count.
- Do not send users from approved pages into unfinished drafts.
- Keep legal and editorial links where they clarify privacy, terms, sourcing or accountability.
