Geo-tagging and local SEO

What image geo-tagging can and cannot do

GPS metadata can help organise image files, but it is not a substitute for accurate page content, business profiles and local listings.

Organise images by location

GPS metadata records where an image was created or assigned. Use it only when the location is accurate and relevant.

Better Image SEO

Title and description metadata give search engines more context about what the image shows — improving image search and page relevance.

Fast Team Workflows

Tag photos once, use them everywhere — website galleries, Google Business Profile posts, social content and location pages.

How to use it well

Geo-tag real photos of your premises, team and work — not stock images. Upload them to your Google Business Profile and location pages, keep filenames descriptive (clinic-reception-jlt-dubai.jpg), and pair the images with a strong Local SEO setup for the full effect.
