01

What AI visibility work is designed to improve

AI visibility work makes the business entity, services, people, products and supporting evidence easier to identify across crawlable sources. It improves clarity and eligibility for discovery; it does not buy or guarantee a citation.

02

What the audit and implementation can include

The scope can include crawl and indexing checks, entity and contact consistency, linked structured data, clearer source pages, llms.txt, internal linking, authorship signals and a monitoring plan for branded discovery across supported search and AI tools.

03

How AI visibility works

Machines need accessible pages, consistent entity facts and passages that answer questions clearly. Those on-site signals become more trustworthy when independent profiles and publications corroborate the same business information.

04

How the work is scoped and priced

The work starts with an audit because the gaps vary by business. Media87 then provides a written scope covering the pages, structured data, profiles, content, monitoring and review responsibilities included in the project.
