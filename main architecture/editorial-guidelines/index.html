PAGE FOCUS Editorial Guidelines

Media87 publishes content to help readers make informed decisions. Each article should have accountable authorship, appropriate evidence, human review and a clear route for corrections.

Discuss your situation →
01

Research and evidence

Use current primary sources, first-party Media87 evidence and qualified expert review. Separate observed facts, approved claims, external evidence, inference and unknowns.

02

Authorship and review

Name the real author and reviewer when appropriate. Do not manufacture delivery experience, tests, dates, quotations or credentials.

03

AI assistance

AI can support research, structure and production, but people remain responsible for facts, originality, sources, permissions and publication judgement.

04

Corrections and material updates

Readers can report a possible error through the contact page. When a substantive correction or update is made, the page should show a meaningful updated date.

BOUNDARIES

These standards apply to Media87 editorial work. Sponsored, affiliate or commercial relationships should be disclosed wherever they could affect a reader’s interpretation.
